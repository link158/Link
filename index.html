📚 Class-wise Syllabus

Class 1: Photography & Graphic Designing

Photography: Camera basics, composition, Rule of Thirds, hands-on shots

Graphic Design: Design principles, Photoshop, Illustrator, poster creation

Class 2: Color Grading & Video Editing

Color Grading: Color correction, hue, saturation, grading demo

Video Editing: Timeline, trimming, transitions, 30-sec edit task

Class 3: Videography & Content Creation

Videography: Frame rates, lighting, camera movement

Content Creation: Audience, platforms, daily content planning

Class 4: Digital Marketing & Social Media

Marketing: SEO, ads, reach, engagement

Social Media: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, scheduling & analytics

Class 5: 3D Animation & Web Development

3D: prisma , modeling, simple animation

Web: HTML, CSS, webpage structure & practice
